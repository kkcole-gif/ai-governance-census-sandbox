AI Governance Census of US Colleges and Universities

Documents through which US colleges and universities govern the use of artificial intelligence, recorded one row per document, with issuing office, dates, a quoted passage, verification status, and any contradiction with another document at the same institution. Sample: 334 institutions drawn from the 2025 Carnegie Classifications.

Institutions often state different rules for AI use across their own documents. Each conflict below is recorded on the document that states it, with the other document named. Conflicts are counted at the row level so they are countable across the census.

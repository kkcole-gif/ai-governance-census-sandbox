## Using the data

The questions below show how to answer common questions with the site or the downloaded files, along with the caution each answer carries.

### How many institutions have a formal policy naming AI?

On the Institutions tab, the fifth count reports institutions with at least one verified document of type 3, Formal policy or regulation. In the CSV files, filter the inventory to Status = Verified and Document type = 3 Formal policy or regulation, then count distinct UNITIDs.

Caution: some institutions are governed only by a system-office policy (a state system regulation with no campus instrument). The institution log names these cases, and published figures report them separately from campus policies.

### How do R1 institutions compare with master's institutions?

Filter by stratum on the Institutions tab, or group the inventory by the Stratum field.

Caution: R1 institutions are a census, while the other strata are random draws. Figures for the drawn strata describe the sample and carry sampling error. Compare proportions, never raw counts, since strata differ in size.

### Which documents did a provost issue?

Choose Provost or Academic Affairs in the issuing-office filter. The census reads the issuing office from the document itself, never from the domain hosting it, so a teaching center page on a provost's website is recorded under the teaching center.

### Where do an institution's own documents disagree?

Open the Contradictions tab, or filter the inventory to rows where Contradiction with another document is not N. Each entry names the other document.

Caution: a contradiction is recorded on the document stating it, so one conflict between two documents appears once or twice depending on how each document reads.

### Has an institution's AI language changed over time?

Use Date issued, Date last revised, and Wave. Wave 1 covers documents issued spring to fall 2023, and Wave 2 covers documents issued or revised from 2024 onward.

Caution: many documents carry no date. The Date basis field states whether a date is stated, read from a file path or page metadata, inferred, or absent, and undated documents are coded Undetermined.

### How do I cite a single document rather than the dataset?

Cite the institution's document directly, using its title, issuing office, and URL from the inventory, and cite the census version through its DOI as the source of the record.

Caution: the quoted passage in Key Framing Language is a short excerpt. A quotation marked Fetch (verify) in Quote source has not yet been checked against the original and should be confirmed before it is quoted elsewhere.

### What does a missing institution mean?

Every drawn institution appears in the institution log, including those with no qualifying document. An institution with the result "No AI governance document located" was searched under the full protocol, and its log entry lists the documents checked. Absence in the census is a recorded result of a search.
